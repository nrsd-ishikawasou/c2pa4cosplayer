## Position of This Document

- Performs Chapter 13 of the Outline Design Document (13-1 to 13-4) on Chapters 1 to 12 of the basic design. It argues that this design achieves the purpose (protecting Users' photo rights and deterring), and declares the known gaps.
- The decisions received are as in the following table (the decisions, items to be investigated, and open items of Design Plan Edition 2, and omissions found in the item breakdown).

|Number|Type|Content|
|---|---|---|
|D-2-3|Decision|The greatest value of the System shall be deterrence (there is no corresponding requirement; it is the purpose of the System, and how each chapter works toward it is argued in 2.2 “How it works toward deterrence”)|
|A-10|Omission found in the item breakdown|Mapping from Design Plan decisions to the design|
|All chapters|—|This document takes the design of all chapters as the object of verification|

- Method of verification: correspondences of numbers (requirements, decisions, destination table) were checked by mechanically extracting numbers from the documents and comparing them. Scenarios, threats, and gaps were checked by following the text of each chapter.
- It reflects the revision of Design Plan Edition 2 (2026-09-29): the System does not collect Users' information and is completed within the Client App. Certificates for C2PA signatures are made on the device and require no certificate authority. Persons posing as the Rights Holder cannot be excluded, and matching clues are shown. No private repository is set up.
- Notation: “[NRSD to confirm]” marks matters NRSD checks and decides (listed in 6).

### 4.3 Legal issues

- The issues of Chapter 12, 7 “Legal Issues (Placed in the Legal Research as Material for Operational Decisions)” (the arrangement on unauthorized practice of law, the presumption under the Electronic Signatures Act, copying for evidence, whether it constitutes a telecommunications business, whether representatives or agents abroad are needed for feedback e-mails, minor Users, the precedence between the license and the Terms of Use) are not guaranteed by the design to meet legal requirements, and are left to NRSD's operational decisions.
- The System does not collect Users' information. All NRSD receives is feedback e-mails Users send themselves, and their handling is published in the Privacy Policy (Chapter 12, 5 “Privacy Policy”). Personal information laws abroad (China's Personal Information Protection Law, the GDPR, etc.) become operational issues only for these e-mails.
- Export control is treated, by using only public standard cryptography and distributing it publicly and free of charge, as a transaction not requiring permission in Japan and as not requiring notification in the United States, and is checked at each release (Chapter 12, DD-12-8“Only public standard cryptography is used, distributed openly and free of charge”).
- The wording of Rights Documents is distributed only after review by experts (Chapter 12, DD-12-10“The wording of Rights Documents is distributed only after expert review”, Design Plan 9.5 “Approach to Governing Law (Reference)”). Countries not yet reviewed are shown only by the link to complete coverage (Chapter 5, 4 “Management of the Wording”).
